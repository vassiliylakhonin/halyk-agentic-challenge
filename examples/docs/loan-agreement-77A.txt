LOAN AGREEMENT No. 77-A
Date: 1 March 2024

Borrower: Astana Logistics LLP
Lender: Example Bank JSC

1. Principal. The Lender provides a loan of 250,000,000 KZT.

2. Interest. Interest accrues at 14.5 per cent per annum on the outstanding
   principal. Interest is calculated on the actual number of days elapsed and a
   365-day year.

3. Interest payment dates. Interest is payable quarterly, on the last business
   day of March, June, September and December.

4. Early repayment. Early repayment in full is permitted with 30 calendar days
   written notice. No early repayment fee applies after the first 12 months.

5. Amendments. This Agreement may be amended only by a written addendum signed
   by both parties.
