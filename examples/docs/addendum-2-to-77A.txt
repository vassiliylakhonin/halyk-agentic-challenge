ADDENDUM No. 2
to Loan Agreement No. 77-A dated 1 March 2024

Date: 15 January 2025
Borrower: Astana Logistics LLP
Lender: Example Bank JSC

The parties agree to amend Loan Agreement No. 77-A as follows.

1. Clause 2 (Interest) is restated: interest accrues at 16.0 per cent per annum
   on the outstanding principal, calculated on the actual number of days elapsed
   and a 365-day year. The amended rate applies from 1 February 2025.

2. Clause 4 (Early repayment) is restated: early repayment in full requires 45
   calendar days written notice and a fee of 0.5 per cent of the amount repaid.

3. All other clauses of Loan Agreement No. 77-A remain in force without change.

This Addendum supersedes Addendum No. 1 dated 10 June 2024 in full.
